HBS CaseCore Sections

From Commercial Bank to Tech Pioneer

In 2007, Mikhail Lomtadze became CEO of Kaspiyskiy Bank (ranked 15th out of 38 banks) and shifted focus from corporate lending to retail. By 2012, despite high profitability, customer satisfaction metrics revealed serious shortcomings.

"We were too focused on profitability, so we lost sight of the customer." Mikhail Lomtadze on 2012 NPS results
Detailed Background & Leadership

Vyacheslav Kim acquired the bank in 2002 after pioneering electronics retail and BNPL installments in Kazakhstan. In 2005, Kim brought in Lomtadze (HBS MBA 2002, Baring Vostok partner). The team rebranded to Kaspi Bank in 2008 and committed to a customer-centric technology mission.

Zero-Fee Payments & NPS as Core KPI

Kaspi solved a major customer pain point by launching digital utility payments with zero commission. However, the February 2014 tenge devaluation caused a bank run, exposing the fragility of customer trust.

"If our customers loved and trusted us, they wouldn't have been at our doorstep asking for their money within 12 hours of a rumor." Mikhail Lomtadze
The Independent NPS Department

Led by Bota Turapbaeva, an independent NPS team makes ~40,000 phone calls monthly within an hour of customer touchpoints. Detractor calls are reviewed monthly by product heads, making NPS the primary corporate KPI.

Starting Simple & Building the Super-App

In December 2014, Kaspi launched its marketplace without a shopping cart (one item per seller), achieving an initial NPS of ~85. In 2017, the super-app launched alongside P2P payments, eventually surpassing Facebook and WhatsApp in daily usage.

MAU & DAU in millions (Exhibit 7). Average DAU/MAU rose from 46% to 59%.

Minimalist Data Collection

Around 2013, Kaspi adopted a strict principle: collect only the data needed to immediately improve user experience. Complex algorithms slow down product cycles, so data collection is kept minimal.

Data Governance & Security

Terms and conditions fit into 3–5 simple sentences. Kaspi never sells user data, and technologies like Kaspi ID protect against fraud.

Merchants, Gov-Tech & London IPO

Kaspi Pay brought low-cost QR payments and instant BNPL to merchants. During the pandemic, Kaspi partnered with the government to digitalize public services, distribute social benefits, and donate ambulances. In October 2020, Kaspi successfully listed on the London Stock Exchange at a $6.5B valuation.
