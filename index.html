HBS CaseCore Sections

From a mid-table bank to a technology company

Kazakhstan is the largest economy in Central Asia ($182B GDP in 2021) and set out in 2012 to reach the global top 30 by 2050. In 2002 Vyacheslav Kim, who had introduced buy-now-pay-later for electronics, bought Kaspiyskiy bank; Baring Vostok became a major shareholder in 2006.

In 2007 Mikhail Lomtadze became CEO of a bank ranked 15th of 38, fighting for corporate clients. He moved it to retail, reasoning that lending to thousands of consumers made more sense than lending to a handful of businesses, and rebranded as Kaspi Bank in 2008. The first NPS survey in 2012 contradicted the strong profit figures.

"We were too focused on profitability, so we lost sight of the customer." Mikhail Lomtadze on the 2012 NPS results
Mission and the pivot

The team concluded that none of them were bankers at heart and that people generally disliked banks. The new mission: be a technology company that improves people's lives, starting from the customer's problem rather than the technology.

Commission-free payments, then NPS as the main KPI

Kaspi's first product targeted a concrete pain: people lost a full day each month in queues and paid fees to settle utility bills. A commission-free digital platform produced recurring customers. Functional silos were replaced by product teams, each with one accountable head, sometimes a 25-year-old.

In February 2014 the tenge fell almost 20%, social-media rumors triggered a run on deposits, and queues formed outside Kaspi outlets. Kaspi handled it, but the lesson was clear.

"If our customers loved and trusted us, they wouldn't have been at our doorstep asking for their money within 12 hours of a rumor." Mikhail Lomtadze
How the NPS machine works

An independent team under Bota Turapbaeva makes about 40,000 calls a month, usually within an hour of a customer touchpoint, with two questions: would you recommend it, and why. Email surveys reach about 5% of customers; calls cover the other 95%. Feedback is sorted into nine categories, and product heads listen to recordings monthly to find root causes. NPS replaced most individual and departmental financial metrics.

Overcoming internal resistance

Lomtadze showed call recordings and social-media screenshots first to senior management, then to 100 key people. After hearing customers directly, staff needed no more convincing.

Start simple, add features, then unify in a super-app

From about 100 monthly proposals, roughly 10 are approved. Each begins as a three-page customer-experience document with ten customer Q&As, followed by an internal prototype test. The marketplace launched in December 2014, nine months after a study trip to Rakuten, with no shopping cart: one item from one seller. It still opened with an NPS of about 85, and by 2016 the three largest electronics retailers had joined.

The 2017 super-app, launched with free P2P transfers, was opposed by almost the whole team because products became interdependent. Lomtadze's answer was to ask "Why not?" By 2020 it was used by 60% of the population and had overtaken Facebook and WhatsApp. Kaspi Travel took 20% of the travel-agency market within three months.

MAU and DAU, millions (Exhibit 7). DAU/MAU rose from 46% to 59%; the Kazakh tech sector averaged 10–20% in 2021.

Collect only the data you will use

Kaspi first gathered as much data as possible, then realized that less was better for the customer and for speed: algorithms built on heavy datasets slow down release cycles. Around 2013 it adopted the rule that data must be usable to improve the user experience.

Consent, fraud and privacy

Consent terms fit into three to five sentences. Kaspi occasionally buys third-party data for personalization and cross-checks profiles against public social-media data to detect fraud, but it never sells data. Banking regulation made privacy "part of our DNA", and Kaspi ID adds protection.

Merchants, government and investors

Merchants. One-click in-store BNPL raised average transaction value by up to 35% and is used by 45% of partner stores. QR payments, priced lowest on the market, handled over 60% of in-store transactions by end-2020; in 2021 Kaspi Pay ran 180,000 of Kazakhstan's 356,000 POS terminals.

Government. Car ownership changes and sole-proprietor registration moved into the app. During the pandemic Kaspi tripled parcel volumes with free delivery, built and donated 100 ambulances, and created an online platform for social benefits.

Investors. The October 2020 London IPO was the second largest on the LSE that year and fourth in Europe, opening at $38 and reaching $47. Lomtadze excluded himself from the employee option plan. Kaspi sets 10–15 KPIs a year and reports quarterly; market capitalization had passed $14B by May 2021.

Can trust travel? Expansion, June 2021

Q2 2021 net income grew 94% to about $240M, a dividend of about $180M was announced, and the full-year profit target rose to about $1B. NPS for new products held near 80, matching established ones. Lomtadze wanted product development to stay in Kazakhstan, with foreign teams focused on marketing and partnerships, and expected market entry to take under twelve months. Azerbaijan had already been entered in 2019 by acquiring marketplaces with two million users.
